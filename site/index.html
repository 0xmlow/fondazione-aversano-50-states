About the field

America, seen through its artists

In September 2026 Fondazione Aversano set out to collect one artist from every state. It ended with 145 artists, 200 works and all fifty states, plus Washington DC, Puerto Rico, Guam and Midway, each acquisition recorded on Ethereum, Tezos or Bitcoin.

The field takes its idea from Donald Judd's Chinati Foundation in Marfa, Texas, where fifteen concrete works stand in a line across the land and two old artillery sheds hold a hundred boxes of aluminum. Here the line becomes the map. Each state is a concrete pavilion open at both ends, set where the state lies. The largest collections stand in Quonset vaulted sheds. You arrive in a reborn artillery shed in Marfa, where an aluminum box for every place leads you to it.

The light follows the Marfa clock. Look south after dark.
